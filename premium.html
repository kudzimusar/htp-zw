How does the Premium preview work?

Each Premium research story gives a short reading window of less than 30 seconds to a non-subscriber. The story then locks and asks the reader to subscribe. Refreshing does not reset that story’s preview.

What does US$5/month include?

Premium analysis, the Premium archive, citation tools, expanded HealthTimes Intelligence, weekly member briefings, saved-reading tools and configurable topic alerts.

Can I choose email or WhatsApp briefings?

Yes. My HealthTimes lets readers choose topics, frequency and delivery channels.

Can I cancel?

The commercial and billing workflow will support account-managed membership controls when the production payment service is connected.
