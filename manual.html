Purpose: this manual is the operating reference for the HealthTimes 2.0 presentation build and the blueprint for the future production implementation.

Public publication

The homepage prioritises lead reporting, top stories, a news brief, latest reporting, most-read treatment, Premium analysis, topic hubs, opinion, trust signals and audience services. On phone and tablet widths, desktop navigation is replaced with an app-style top bar and bottom navigation.

Article tools

  • reading progress;
  • author, beat, date and reporting context;
  • save to reading list;
  • WhatsApp-first sharing plus Facebook, X and LinkedIn;
  • direct WhatsApp comment with article context;
  • Ask HealthTimes contextual prompts;
  • correction request;
  • related coverage.

HealthTimes Premium

Premium is positioned at US$5/month and includes deeper analysis, member briefings, expanded HealthTimes Intelligence, saved reading and topic alerts. An introductory 30-second preview allows sampling without trapping the user interface.

After the preview expires, protected reporting locks while navigation, public stories, search and other site functions remain usable. The membership sheet can always be dismissed with its close control, Not now, backdrop click or Escape.

Ask HealthTimes

HealthTimes Intelligence is a journalism research and discovery assistant. It can summarise the current story, explain significance, translate complex reporting into plain language, find related coverage and search the bundled HealthTimes reporting dataset.

Health safety: it does not diagnose, prescribe medication, provide doses or replace qualified clinical care.

Email, WhatsApp and reader preferences

My HealthTimes lets readers choose topics, delivery frequency and channels. Supported presentation channels are email, WhatsApp and browser notifications. The public HealthTimes WhatsApp desk is +263 77 628 0754, with +263 772 679 680 also listed by HealthTimes.

Production delivery must add verified subscriber identity, consent, unsubscribe state and real provider integrations.

HealthTimes Newsroom

The Newsroom is a separate administration surface for assignments, drafts, review, publishing, Premium, AI governance, audience channels and staff roles. Staff sessions are account-specific. There is no impersonation control: a user must sign out before another account can authenticate.

Editorial and operational roles

The role model includes Publisher / Owner, Editor-in-Chief, Managing Editor, Section Editor, News Editor, Reporter / Journalist, Health / Science Editor, Fact Checker, Copy Editor, Multimedia Editor, Social Editor, Newsletter Editor, Commercial Manager, Subscriber Manager and Analyst.

Reporters can create and work on their own or assigned stories but cannot publish freely. Editorial leadership can review and publish. Commercial and subscriber functions are separated from editorial-copy authority.

Editorial workflow

Idea → Assignment → Draft → Source verification → Fact check → Health/science review when needed → Copy edit → Ready → Scheduled → Published → Correction/Update → Archived.

The presentation compresses some states but preserves the authority boundary: work must move through review, publishing is restricted by role, and publication/update actions are recorded in the newsroom audit history.

Production transition

Before operational launch, replace browser-local presentation services with managed authentication and MFA, server-side RBAC, an authoritative CMS/database, revision history, server-side Premium entitlement, payment processing, subscriber consent and delivery providers, privacy-aware analytics and server-side AI retrieval over the authoritative CMS.

Troubleshooting

If a page returns 404, confirm the requested file exists and GitHub Pages has deployed the current main branch. The build includes explicit favicon and manifest assets plus a custom 404 page.

The browser message Unchecked runtime.lastError: Could not establish connection. Receiving end does not exist. is commonly produced by browser extensions. First-party HealthTimes network requests should be checked separately.
