Desktop and mobile are separate presentations

Desktop uses a multi-column editorial layout, full institutional footer, ad inventory and wider article context. Phone/tablet widths use a compact native-news feed, app masthead, bottom navigation and persistent sheets. The desktop footer is intentionally removed on mobile; those destinations live under More.

Reader accounts and appearance

The public profile icon supports Sign in, Create account and reader profile. Reader state keeps Premium status, saved reading, reading history and theme choice together.

Premium presentation reader: reader@healthtimes.co.zw / HealthTimes#Reader26.

Light, Dark and System themes are available through account/appearance controls.

HealthTimes Premium

Premium is priced at US$5/month. Each Premium story receives its own non-subscriber reading allowance. A small notice appears after a few seconds, the warning strengthens around twenty seconds, and protected research locks automatically at thirty seconds. The subscription sheet opens automatically at expiry.

Closing the subscription sheet never unlocks the research, but readers can continue navigating public HealthTimes. Refreshing does not reset the allowance for that reader/story. Premium members bypass the gate and gain citation tools, briefings, saved reading, deeper HealthTimes Intelligence and fewer advertising interruptions.

Listen to this story

Article pages include a compact speaker icon for readers who prefer audio, are multitasking, or benefit from text-to-speech accessibility. Tapping the icon starts narration; tapping it again pauses, and another tap resumes. The reader prioritises higher-quality English voices exposed by the operating system or browser.

Premium rules apply equally to listening: narration stops immediately when a non-member’s Premium preview expires and protected paragraphs are no longer narratable.

Advertising

HealthTimes retains its paid commercial inventory. The source publication’s HOSPAZ campaign is represented as the initial campaign. Advertising follows the navigation hierarchy on every page: it never sits above or overlaps the primary app navigation. On mobile home it is placed naturally inside the editorial feed; on other public pages it enters the content flow after the app chrome.

Newsroom → Advertising lets authorized staff manage commercial inventory while remaining separated from editorial-copy authority.

Ask HealthTimes

HealthTimes Intelligence helps readers summarise a story, explain why it matters, find related reporting and navigate HealthTimes coverage. It remains a journalism/research tool, not a doctor: it must not diagnose, prescribe medication or provide dosing.

HealthTimes Newsroom OS

The Newsroom is now a dedicated staff application rather than an administration panel attached to the public website. When staff are signed out, they see only the Newsroom authentication gateway. A successful sign-in removes that gateway completely and opens the role-shaped workspace. The staff session persists across refresh until sign-out or access revocation.

The authenticated shell is organised around day-to-day newsroom work: My Newsroom, Editorial, Content, Distribution, Intelligence, Commercial, Organisation and System. Navigation changes according to the signed-in staff member’s capabilities.

The Newsroom visual system deliberately uses dark navy navigation, warm paper work surfaces, HealthTimes teal and restrained operational status colours. It avoids glassmorphism and decorative AI-gradient styling.

Role-shaped workspaces

Reporter / Journalist: active assignments, due-today work, drafts, submissions waiting for editorial review, personal calendar and story workspace. Reporters can create, autosave, manually save, reopen and submit their own work but cannot publish.

Editor-in-Chief: review queue, ready-to-publish stories, overdue assignments, fact-check and specialist review, corrections, desk activity, trending signals and publication decisions.

Newsletter / Audience: newsletter editions, WhatsApp briefing queue, social distribution, reader interests and editorial scheduling.

Commercial Manager: advertising, Premium product and subscriber operations. Editorial story-editing navigation is intentionally unavailable.

Publisher / Owner: organisation-wide editorial inventory, staff/access, Premium, campaigns, security and audit context.

Additional configured roles include Managing Editor, Section Editor, News Editor, Health/Science Editor, Fact Checker, Copy Editor, Multimedia Editor, Social Editor, Subscriber Manager and Analyst.

Stories, assignments and review

The target lifecycle is:

Pitch → Approved → Assigned → Reporting → Draft → Submitted → Fact check → Health / Science review → Copy edit → Editor review → Ready → Scheduled → Published → Updated / Corrected → Archived.

The story workspace provides headline, standfirst, body, sources/references, internal reporting notes, assignment, deadline, desk, section, country, region, Public/Premium access, SEO fields, distribution choices, scheduling and advertising settings.

While writing, the workspace shows Saving… and Saved states. Autosaves and manual saves create a local version trail. Internal editorial comments stay inside the Newsroom and never appear on the public story.

The Assignments Desk records reporter, desk, deadline, priority, assigned editor and reporting notes. Reporters can advance their own assignment through accepted/reporting/drafting/submitted states. Editors use Review Queue to advance work through evidence and specialist review, request changes and publish when authorised.

Changing a mapped public article to HealthTimes Premium updates the same public access override used by the 30-second reader contract.

Staff, permissions and security

Staff & Access shows name, email, role, desk, beat, status, last login and MFA readiness. Authorised leadership can invite staff, change role, revoke sessions and revoke access. Revocation requires explicit confirmation and generates an audit entry.

Authority is defined by granular capabilities such as story.edit_own, story.edit_all, story.publish, premium.assign, ads.approve, staff.invite and staff.revoke. Roles are collections of those capabilities.

The Security workspace shows the current session, other session controls, account role and MFA/password readiness. Production must replace browser-local credentials and sessions with managed identity, MFA, server-side RBAC and immutable audit logging.

PWA, Google Play and App Store

HealthTimes is configured as an installable PWA with a manifest and service worker. The repository includes a Google Play Trusted Web Activity/Bubblewrap scaffold and a Capacitor configuration for a future signed iOS wrapper.

See Mobile App Packaging Guide → and Store Listing Draft →.

Browser certification

Client-review releases are checked in Chromium/Playwright across public and Newsroom journeys. Newsroom gates include the authentication gateway, session persistence, reporter autosave/reopen/submit, editor review/publish, commercial/editorial separation, staff invite/session/access revocation and responsive layout integrity.

Public gates continue to cover mobile/desktop presentation, Premium timing and persistence, ad hierarchy, article listening, archive parity and PWA availability.

Production transition

Before launch, replace browser-local identity, Newsroom stories, assignments, revisions, Premium, story overrides, advertising metrics and preferences with secure backend services. Add managed staff/reader authentication and MFA, server-side permissions, authoritative CMS/database, payment processing, server/edge entitlement, consent-aware messaging, production analytics, immutable audit logging, server-side AI retrieval and signed mobile builds.

Global newsroom expansion

The Newsroom taxonomy is already prepared for Global Health, Africa, Southern Africa, East Africa, West Africa, Central Africa, North Africa, Zimbabwe, Research, Policy, Investigations and Health Business. This supports HealthTimes becoming a global health publication with Africa as its strongest reporting authority.
