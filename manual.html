Desktop and mobile are separate presentations

Desktop uses a multi-column editorial layout, full institutional footer, ad inventory and wider article context. Phone/tablet widths use a compact native-news feed, app masthead, bottom navigation and persistent sheets. The desktop footer is intentionally removed on mobile; those destinations live under More.

Reader accounts and appearance

The public profile icon supports Sign in, Create account and reader profile. Reader state keeps Premium status, saved reading, reading history and theme choice together.

Premium presentation reader: reader@healthtimes.co.zw / HealthTimes#Reader26.

Light, Dark and System themes are available through account/appearance controls.

HealthTimes Premium

Premium is priced at US$5/month. Each Premium story receives its own non-subscriber reading allowance. A small notice appears after a few seconds, the warning strengthens around twenty seconds, and protected research locks automatically at thirty seconds. The subscription sheet opens automatically at expiry.

Closing the subscription sheet never unlocks the research, but readers can continue navigating public HealthTimes. Refreshing does not reset the allowance for that reader/story. Premium members bypass the gate and gain citation tools, briefings, saved reading, deeper HealthTimes Intelligence and fewer advertising interruptions.

Listen to this story

Article pages include a compact speaker icon for readers who prefer audio, are multitasking, or benefit from text-to-speech accessibility. Tapping the icon starts narration; tapping it again pauses, and another tap resumes. The reader prioritises higher-quality English voices exposed by the operating system or browser, including enhanced, premium, natural and neural voices when available.

Premium rules apply equally to listening: narration stops immediately when a non-member’s Premium preview expires, protected paragraphs are no longer narratable, and the reader is prompted to subscribe to continue. See HealthTimes Listen guide →.

Advertising

HealthTimes retains its paid commercial inventory. The source publication’s HOSPAZ campaign is represented as the initial campaign. Advertising follows the navigation hierarchy on every page: it never sits above or overlaps the primary app navigation. On mobile home it is placed naturally inside the editorial feed; on other public pages it enters the content flow after the app chrome.

Newsroom → Advertising lets authorized staff manage advertiser, campaign, creative, placement, status, review, disclosure and schedule. Commercial staff remain separated from editorial-copy authority.

Ask HealthTimes

HealthTimes Intelligence helps readers summarise a story, explain why it matters, find related reporting and navigate HealthTimes coverage. It remains a journalism/research tool, not a doctor: it must not diagnose, prescribe medication or provide dosing.

Newsroom operations

Newsroom covers stories, assignments, calendar, review, media, authors, topics, breaking coverage, Premium, Advertising, AI, audience, newsletters, WhatsApp, subscribers, analytics, staff/roles, settings and migration parity.

Workflow: Idea → Assignment → Draft → Source verification → Fact check → Health/science review → Copy edit → Ready/Scheduled → Published → Correction/Update → Archive.

Authorized editors can set a mapped story to Public or HealthTimes Premium. Commercial roles can manage ads/subscriber products but cannot silently edit editorial copy.

PWA, Google Play and App Store

HealthTimes is configured as an installable PWA with a manifest and service worker. The repository includes a Google Play Trusted Web Activity/Bubblewrap scaffold and a Capacitor configuration for a future signed iOS wrapper.

See Mobile App Packaging Guide → and Store Listing Draft →.

Browser certification

The client-review release is checked in Chromium/Playwright at 375, 430, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 widths. Gates cover layout overflow/overlap, stable sheets, accounts, themes, Premium timing, ads, article listening and its Premium boundary, Newsroom Premium/Advertising/RBAC and PWA assets.

The final certification record is stored in docs/UAT_CERTIFICATION.md and workflow results in GitHub Actions.

Production transition

Before launch, replace browser-local identity, Premium, story overrides, advertising metrics and preferences with secure backend services. Add payment processing, server/edge entitlement, managed authentication/MFA, CMS/database revisions, consent-aware messaging, production analytics, server-side AI retrieval and signed mobile builds.
