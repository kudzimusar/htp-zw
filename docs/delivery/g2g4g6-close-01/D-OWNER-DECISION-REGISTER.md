# Owner and client decision register: HealthTimes 2.0 launch

**Prepared:** 2026-11-14. **Every decision below is OPEN.** The recommendations are proposals only; nothing here has been approved.
**Audience:** HealthTimes Owner and Publisher, plus the named client contacts.

**How to use it.** For each row, record the decision, the decision-maker and the date, then return the register. Rows marked **Launch-blocking** stop the Reader-only launch until decided.

| ID | Decision | Why it matters | Recommendation (not approved) | Blocks | Decided by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | **Newsroom role holders:** name the Journalist(s), Editor(s) and Publisher, plus a backup Publisher | Native publishing and the freshness acceptance test need a real Publisher. Separation of duties needs at least two people. | At least 1 Publisher, 1 Editor and 1 Journalist, all distinct, plus 1 backup Publisher | **Launch-blocking** (G2 continuity) | Owner |
| D-02 | **WordPress content freeze and cut-over:** the freeze window, the cut-over date, who owns the migration, and whether WordPress stays online read-only | Stops content being lost between the last sync and cut-over | Freeze of at most 48 hours; the G2/G3 specialist as migration owner; WordPress kept read-only for at least 12 months | **Launch-blocking** | Owner + Publisher |
