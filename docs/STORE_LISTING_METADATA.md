## Full description

HealthTimes Zimbabwe brings trusted health reporting, policy analysis, research and public-health news into a mobile-first reading experience designed for Zimbabwe and the wider African health community.

Follow breaking health developments, HIV/AIDS, public health, health policy, financing, medical research, disease outbreaks, health business and global health. Save stories, follow the topics that matter to you, choose email or WhatsApp briefings, switch between light and dark themes, and use Ask HealthTimes to understand the context behind published reporting.

HealthTimes Premium gives members access to deeper research, policy analysis, citation tools, member briefings and expanded health-intelligence features.

HealthTimes is a journalism and information product. It does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe treatment or replace professional medical care.
